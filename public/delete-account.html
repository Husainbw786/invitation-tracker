Delete your Invitations account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Invitations and sign in.
  2. On your events screen, tap the gear at the top right to open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my account, confirm with your password (or with Google), and tap Delete for good.

Open Invitations

Without the app

Email husainhackerrank@gmail.com from the email address you sign in with, and ask us to delete your account. We delete it within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (name, email and password).
  • Every event you own, with all its guests, RSVP links and answers. This is gone for your helpers too.
  • Your place on events you help with (those events stay with their owners).

Nothing is kept after deletion, and it cannot be undone.
