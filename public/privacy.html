Invitations

Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

Invitations is an app for keeping guest lists for weddings, birthdays, Eid and other events: who got their invitation, who is coming, and how many. This page says what it stores, who can see it, and how to delete it.

What we store

  • Your account: your name and email address, and your password (kept by Google Firebase, never readable by us). If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email with the app.
  • Your guest lists: what you or your helpers type in or pick from your phone's contacts: names, cities, phone numbers, how many people, tags, notes and invitation status.
  • RSVP answers: when a guest opens their private RSVP link, their answer (coming or not, how many, which functions and an optional message) is saved to your event.
  • On your device only: your light or dark choice and the last event you opened, so the app opens where you left it.

The app only sees the contacts you pick yourself. It does not read your whole contact list.

Who can see it

  • An event's list is seen only by its owner and the people the owner adds as helpers.
  • Someone with a guest's private RSVP link sees the event's name, date and place, and that guest's own answer. Nothing else on the list.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking.

Where it is kept

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore). The website is served by Vercel. Both act only to run the app.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Settings → Delete my account. This deletes your sign-in, every event you own with all its guests and RSVP answers, and removes you from events you help with. See how to delete your account, including how to ask us to do it if you cannot open the app.

Deleting a single event or guest in the app removes it straight away.

Children

The app is meant for adults planning events. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
